Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28.

MarsAI backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own server and Google account.

  • Access: the app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can see and manage only the files it creates.
  • Data: backup data is encrypted on the owner's server before upload; Google Drive stores only encrypted blobs.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising or analytics.
  • Retention and deletion: backups follow the owner's retention policy and can be deleted at any time from Google Drive; access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: github.com/MarsSall.
